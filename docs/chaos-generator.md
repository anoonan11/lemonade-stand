# Chaos Generator Functionality

To make the game more challenging, a chaos generator is going to be implemented. Only one can happen per day.

## Chaos Scenarios

### Pestulance
Description: all of your lemon inventory rots and you have zero lemons to start the day.
Odds: 15% chance

### Thunderstorm
Description: no people pass by your lemonade stand for the day.
Odds: 20% chance

### Windstorm 
Description: the day is windy and 25% less people show up than the typical calculation and 50% of your sugar blows away for the day.
Odds: 10% chance

### Lemon TikTok Trend
Description: People are using lemons to bleach their hair as a cheap hairstyling hack. You're limited to buying a single pound of lemons due to supply chain issues for the next 3 days.
Odds: 5% chance

### Bad review -> for the rest of the game play, 50% less people buy from your stand.
Description: Sour Grapes, the owner of a competing juice stand, Grapeade, leaves you a bad review. 50% less people buy from your stand for the rest of the gameplay.
Odds: 1% chance
