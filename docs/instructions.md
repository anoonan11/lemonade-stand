# Lemonade Stand Game

The player is an enterprising lemonade stand owner. The game entails acquiring resources (ice, cups, lemons, sugar) and selling lemonade. You start out with some beginning capital, and your score is your current amount of money. If you end a day with no money and not enough inventory on hand to make lemonade, you’re bankrupt! The game is over.

You’re going to build a game simulating running a lemonade stand. Have some fun with it!

**The setup**

Each day is an opportunity to sell lemonade. Acquire inventory for the day(s) ahead, set a price point for your lemonade, and then simulate the day! You cannot make changes during the day—if you run out of inventory to make lemonade, the day is over. Any ice left over at the end melts and is gone, the rest of your resources carry forward.

**Recommendations and things to avoid**

Fiddling with the “physics”. Statistical functions, like taking the setup for the day and producing a somewhat randomized outcome for lemonade sold, can be a bit of a rabbit hole. To make a fun and challenging game can be finicky. We won’t be grading based on the actual playability, so don’t spend too much time making the outcomes realistic. If you’re inclined, you can simply point out in the README how those functions are set up and what would need to be done to optimize them for playability.

Remember this is a volume game, i.e. we want to see you accomplish a solid amount of work. Using AI and planning ahead can help you to do so. Try not to get stuck—if you do get stuck on something, consider whether you can note a TODO or similar commentary in the README and move on.

**Extensions to consider**

This is a list of ideas and areas where you could go deeper. **Please don’t interpret this as a list of mandatory things or any indication of how far you should make it.** It’s just meant to help jog your thinking for what you might want to build.

Technical areas:

- UI: It’s up to you to decide how much or little UI you want to build. You can skip on a GUI entirely and make a text-based game, you could choose to show off your design and frontend skills, or something in the middle.
- Testing: how do you test your program? How deep do you want to go?
- Complexity and code design: what extensions do you anticipate? Did you pay care to certain areas of the design and architecture you felt were important?
- Data model: how did you model things? Did you choose to go particularly deep here, or were there specific choices you made? Why?

Game areas:

- What other variables affect the outcomes for a day of selling? Upcoming weather? Holidays? A user tweak-able recipe?
- What financial or investment decisions can I make as a player? Is it cheaper to buy in bulk? Are there upgrades or equipment that might help me?

Have fun! We’re excited to see what you build.
